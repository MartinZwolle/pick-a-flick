Pick a Flick M9.2 — more discovery, less popularity

- Normal Film Night no longer asks TMDb for popularity.desc.
- Uses vote_average.desc with a modest vote-count floor: quality guard, not popularity ranking.
- Searches up to six TMDb pages and builds a pool of up to 60 viable movies before local ranking.
- The local taste score remains the main ranking signal.
- Movies shown to the selected viewers in their last 3 movie nights receive a rotation penalty.
- 'Nu niet' receives the strongest repeat penalty among dismissed candidates.
- Finalists get only a light penalty, so a genuine contender can return.
- Chosen movies get a strong repeat penalty; watched/seen filtering remains in force.
- Gems / surprise / era keep their own semantics, but benefit from recent-candidate rotation.
- No migration required. Apply over M9.1.
